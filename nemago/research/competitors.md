# NemaGo — Competitor Notes

Researched Oct 21, 2026. All info comes from each company's own website plus public review sites (Trustpilot, BBB, DealerRater). Review quotes are copied word for word (some cut short with "...") and link to where I found them.

**Quick heads up before the table:**

- **Wrench owns YourMechanic** (bought it in 2022, [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2022/mobile-car-repair-startup-wrench-acquires-another-competitor-swoops-up-yourmechanic/)). So these two are really one company with two brands. Some YourMechanic reviews even say "Wrench."
- **Spiffy has mostly moved away from serving drivers.** Its website now says it is "a B2B automotive service software company, not a service provider itself" ([Spiffy FAQ](https://www.getspiffy.com/faqs)). It sells software and vans to car dealerships and does fleet maintenance.
- **Almost nobody posts real dollar prices.** Most of them say "upfront quote" but you only see the number after you enter your car and zip code.

## 3. What does nobody serve well in this market?

**Nobody gives you a price you can actually trust AND gets someone to you fast.**

- The mobile mechanic apps (Wrench, YourMechanic, AutoNation) say "upfront price," but people keep saying the price changes, or they pay a $150 diagnostic fee and still don't get a clear answer.
- RepairPal is the only one really focused on fair prices, but it just sends you to a regular shop, and the shops often don't stick to the estimate.
- All of them are **scheduled** service. You book a time window and wait. Nobody is built for the driver who is **stuck right now** on the side of the road. People complain about no-shows and 4-hour waits even for planned visits.
- When something goes wrong, it's hard to reach a real person or get a refund.
- Spiffy basically left the everyday driver behind to sell to dealerships.

**So the gap for NemaGo:** a driver with a car problem *right now* who wants (1) a clear diagnosis before paying, (2) a locked price that can't change, and (3) a real mechanic who actually shows up fast. Nobody does all three.
